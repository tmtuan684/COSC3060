Accessibility

What is accessibility?

Practices of making websites usable to various kinds of people, particularly those who have difficulty in using information technology.

Those with disabilities, specifically seeing or hearing or touching or cognitive impair, are in need for support in accessing IT applications.

There are assistive technologies (AT) to assist people in using IT product, particularly Web applictions.
